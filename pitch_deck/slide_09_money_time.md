# Money and time

## Partnering on fill-finish is the cheapest path.

- **Lean build** (acquired facility, partnered fill-finish, 351(k)): about **$120M, ~4 years**
- **Full build** (greenfield, own fill-finish, 351(a)): about **$380M, 8–10 years**
- Partnering on fill-finish alone saves about 12 months
- **Seed: $3–8M** answers the deciding unknowns first: freedom to operate, a bench-scale process, and real purification recovery
- Non-dilutive: an NIH small-business grant application to rebuild the plant is being prepared
