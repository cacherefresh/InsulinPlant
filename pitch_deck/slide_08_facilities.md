# The growing buildings exist

## The cannabis boom overbuilt automated greenhouses. Some sold cheap.

- **Aurora Sun**, Medicine Hat, Alberta: about $200M reported spent. Sold in 2023 for an undisclosed base price plus up to C$15M contingent
- **Former iBio plant-made pharma facility**, Bryan, Texas: sold to the Texas A&M University System for $8.5M in June 2024
- The first question for any building: can it meet APHIS confinement?
