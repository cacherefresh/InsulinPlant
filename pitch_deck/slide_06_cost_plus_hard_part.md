# Cost Plus already does one of the hardest steps

- **22,000 sq ft** sterile fill-finish facility in Deep Ellum, Dallas, opened 2023
- **Aseptic robotic filling lines**, built to switch between products quickly
- First commercial batches were injectables: epinephrine and norepinephrine
- Built for about **$11M**
- The question to ask, not assume: **can these lines fill insulin, and what would qualifying them take?**
