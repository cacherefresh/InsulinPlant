# The problem is upstream

## Three companies make over 90% of the world's insulin.

- Whoever makes the drug substance, the insulin molecule itself, sets the floor on price
- Lowering the markup helps. Lowering the cost of making the molecule is the next step
- A second, independent source of drug substance changes who sets that floor

> Don't claim what anyone pays for insulin today. The concentration fact is enough.
