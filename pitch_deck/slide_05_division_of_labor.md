# Division of labor

## We don't reinvent the wheel. Each side does what it already knows.

- **Ours: Grow.** Safflower under USDA APHIS confinement
- **Ours: Extract.** Separate the protein-carrying oil bodies from the seed (Botaneco license)
- **Ours: Purify and convert.** Bring it to drug-substance spec, ready to hand off
- **Cost Plus: Fill-finish.** Sterile vials on aseptic filling lines it already runs
- **Cost Plus: Distribute.** Through its own pharmacy

> Purification sits on our side of the line. A fill-finish plant receives finished drug substance. Say this before anyone asks.
