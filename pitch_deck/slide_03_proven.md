# Already proven in people

## Safflower insulin matched Humulin in a 2009 human trial.

- SemBioSys's SBS-1000: Phase I/II, **23 healthy volunteers**
- Bioequivalent to Humulin S on total insulin exposure, peak insulin concentration, and total glucose infused
- 2007: after an FDA meeting, the company reported no issues preventing safflower from being used to make it
- Today the route is a **351(k) biosimilar**, because insulin has been regulated as a biologic since March 2020

!claim

> These are company-reported results. Say "company-reported" if asked.
