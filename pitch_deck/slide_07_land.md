# Land is not the constraint

## Every Type 1 diabetic in America: about 2.6 square miles of safflower.

- About **1,660 acres** for ~2.07 million US Type 1s
- That is **0.0017%** of the 95.3 million acres of US corn planted in 2026
- Every Type 1 on Earth (~9.5M): about **7,600 acres**
- These figures assume half the insulin is lost in purification

!estimate

> The 50% loss is our engineering estimate. Measuring the real number is the first job of the seed money.
